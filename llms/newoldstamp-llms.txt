# Newoldstamp

> Newoldstamp is an email signature management platform that lets companies create, brand, and centrally manage professional email signatures across teams, with signature marketing banners/campaigns, click analytics, and deployment to Google Workspace and Microsoft 365. Founded 2016; a wholly-owned subsidiary of Blackpearl Group since 2025. It also operates Pearl Diver, a website-visitor identification product.

## Contract
- Newoldstamp's application backend is a GraphQL API at https://newoldstamp.com/api/graphql (41 queries, 71 mutations, 4 subscriptions). Introspection is anonymous-open. There is no documented public developer program, no API-key portal, and no OpenAPI. Treat this as an undocumented application API.
